# Game Design Document (GDD)

## 1. Executive Summary
- **Title:** Alien Ocean Survival (Working Title)
- **Genre:** 2D Open-World Survival / Crafting / Narrative Exploration
- **Target Audience:** Fans of *Subnautica*, *Terraria*, *Don't Starve*, *Dave the Diver*
- **Platform:** PC (Windows, Linux, macOS)
- **Resolution Target:** 1920×1080 native canvas, rendered crisply up to 4K displays.

## 2. Core Gameplay Loop
1. **Explore:** Swim/walk across the shallow reefs, trench depths, and floating island biomes.
2. **Gather:** Harvest alien flora, mine underwater minerals, sample fauna tissue.
3. **Craft:** Build tools, survival gear, oxygen upgrades, habitat modules, and vehicles.
4. **Survive:** Manage Oxygen, Health, Hunger, Thirst, and Body Temperature.
5. **Uncover:** Follow alien signal beacons, decipher ancient relics, and piece together the planet's story.

## 3. World & Biomes
- **Alien Shallows:** Bioluminescent coral reefs, docile filter-feeders, abundance of basic minerals.
- **Kelp Forests:** Dense towering alien kelp, predatory stalkers, fiber & organic compounds.
- **Deep Trenches:** Pitch-black depths, extreme pressure, volatile vents, rare end-game ores.
- **Floating Islands:** Buoyant landmasses suspended by gas-sacs, terrestrial flora & story ruins.

## 4. Key Systems
- **Fluid Movement:** Seamless transition between top-down walking on land/habitats and 8-directional swimming underwater.
- **Dynamic Day/Night Cycle:** 24-minute real-time day cycle affecting fauna behavior and bioluminescence visibility.
- **Data-driven Content:** Items, recipes, creatures, and dialogue defined in `.tres` / JSON resources.
