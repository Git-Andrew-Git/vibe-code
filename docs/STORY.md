# Story Bible & Narrative Framework

## High Concept
You are **Dr. Aris Thorne**, sole survivor of the survey vessel *Astraea*, which broke apart in high orbit above **Kepler-186f (known locally as 'Pelagios')** — a vast ocean planet dotted with fleeting archipelagos.

## Narrative Themes
- Isolation vs. Adaptability
- Harmony with an alien ecosystem vs. Exploitation
- The mystery of an ancient civilization that mastered ocean manipulation before vanishing.

## Key Story Beats
1. **Act I — The Crash & The Shallows**
   - Escape pod touches down in the temperate shallows.
   - Survival basics: manufacture oxygen tank, construct rudimentary spear, fix pod radio.
   - Pick up an encrypted SOS signal from another module of the *Astraea*.

2. **Act II — Into the Depths**
   - Construct the sub-mersible *Nautila* to dive past 200m depth.
   - Discover the "Abyssal Structures" — non-human architecture made of living coral-metal alloy.
   - Discover that the crash wasn't an accident: a planetary planetary defense grid targeted the ship.

3. **Act III — The Cure & The Ascent**
   - Contract an indigenous alien spore infection ("Phyto-blight").
   - Synthesize a cure using rare deep-sea flora extracts.
   - Re-activate the Alien Spire to disable the grid and launch an emergency distress beacon.
